                                         # Customer-Support-Performance-Analysis


                                                  ## Project Overview

Customer support performance directly affects customer experience, satisfaction, and the efficiency of service teams.

In this project, I used **PostgreSQL and Power BI** to analyze customer support ticket data, identify service areas requiring attention, and understand the factors affecting support performance.

Rather than simply building charts, the analysis focused on answering business questions and turning support data into clear, actionable insights.


                                                ## Business Problem

The business wanted to understand:

* Which support categories take the longest to resolve?
* Which regions have the highest escalation rates?
* Which ticket categories are reopened most often?
* How does first response time relate to customer satisfaction (CSAT)?
* Which areas of the support process may require management attention?


                                               ## Project Goal

The objective of this analysis was to evaluate customer support performance, identify operational problem areas, and provide insights that could help improve resolution efficiency, reduce unnecessary escalations and reopenings, and improve the overall customer experience.

                                                        ## Key Findings

### Delivery & Shipping Takes Longest to Resolve

Delivery & Shipping tickets recorded the highest average resolution time at approximately **69 hours**, making them the slowest category in the analysis.

This suggests that delivery-related issues may require closer investigation to understand the causes of longer resolution times.

### North America Has the Highest Escalation Rate

North America recorded the highest escalation rate at approximately **7%**, higher than the other regions analyzed.

This could indicate that support issues in this region are more likely to require escalation.

### Account Access Is Reopened Most Often

Account Access tickets had the highest reopen rate at approximately **11%**.

Repeated reopening may indicate that some issues are not being fully resolved during the initial interaction.

### Faster First Responses Do Not Clearly Improve CSAT

The analysis compared **first response time with customer satisfaction (CSAT)**.

The scatter plot does not show a strong or clear relationship between the two variables. Customers who received faster responses did not consistently record higher CSAT scores.

This suggests that response speed alone may not be enough to improve customer satisfaction. Other factors, such as the quality of the resolution and the nature of the issue, may also influence how customers rate their experience.

                                                  ## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommend:

* The causes of long **Delivery & Shipping** resolution times should be investigated.
* Review support processes contributing to high escalation rates in **North America**.
* Investigate why **Account Access** issues are frequently reopened.
* Avoid relying on first response speed alone as a measure of customer satisfaction.
* Combine response time with resolution quality, issue category, and customer feedback when evaluating support performance.
* Monitor these KPIs regularly to identify changes in support performance.
